ExiEngine examples

Small, complete games that use only src/index.js. Run npm run dev and open them from here.

  • Platformer — Tiled map import, tile collision bodies, gravity, jump buffering, camera follow with bounds, HUD overlay.
  • Top-down arena — axis input, static wall bodies, trigger pickups, A* chasing enemies, particles, camera shake, a torch whose light is blocked by the walls (shadows).
  • Normal-mapped lighting — LightLayer with a normal buffer: generated brick and orb normal maps, a torch on the pointer, toggling normal maps, orb shadows and light height.
  • Physics sandbox — RigidBodyWorld: boxes, circles and convex polygons with rotation, friction, restitution; a standing pyramid, a joint bridge, pendulum, motorized windmill, piston, a flipper with angle limits and a drivable car on wheel joints; click to drop shapes.
  • Space shooter — pooled bullets and enemies, seeded waves, circle collision, particle explosions, camera shake, parallax stars, a high score in SaveStore; keyboard, gamepad and touch.
  • Endless runner — one-button jumping with variable height, coyote time and buffering, procedural obstacles and coins, ramping speed, parallax layers, tweened pickups.
  • Match-3 puzzle — tap, drag or keyboard swaps, tweened clears and cascades awaited through Tween callbacks, a StateMachine that blocks input while the board settles, reshuffles, shapes as well as colors.
  • UI widgets — Button, Slider, TextInput (IME/mobile through a hidden input), ScrollView, Checkbox, Dropdown and Stack with pointer, wheel and keyboard focus.
  • UI menu — overlay scene, pointer and keyboard focus, tweened buttons, StateMachine screens, responsive layout.
